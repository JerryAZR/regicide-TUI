# Regicide - Solo Rules

---

## Overview

> "A sinister corruption has spread throughout the four great kingdoms, blackening the hearts of once-loved Kings and Queens and those that protect them. As brave adventurers you must work together using the special powers of your champions and animal companions. Overthrow the corrupted monarchs, purge them of their darkness and add them to your ranks so that life can be brought to the land once more."

## Aim of the Game

Regicide is a cooperative game where you work to defeat 12 powerful enemies. You take turns to play cards to attack the enemy and once enough damage is dealt, the enemy is defeated. You win when the last King is defeated. But beware! Each turn the enemy strikes back. You will discard cards to satisfy the damage and if you can't discard enough, you lose!

---

## Setup

1. **Build the Castle Deck:**
   - Shuffle the 4 Kings and put them facedown in a pile on the table.
   - Shuffle the 4 Queens and put them facedown on top of the Kings.
   - Shuffle the 4 Jacks and put them facedown on top of the Queens.
   - This is the Castle deck.
   - Place the Castle deck in the centre of the table and turn the top card face up to reveal a Jack. This will become the current enemy.

2. **Build the Tavern Deck:**
   - Shuffle together all the cards numbered 2 to 10 with the 4 Animal Companions to form your Tavern deck.
   - Place the two Jesters to the side (they will be used for their special power).

3. **Deal Cards:**
   - Deal yourself a hand of 8 cards.
   - The Tavern deck is where cards are dealt and drawn from.
   - Place any discarded cards into a discard pile beside the Tavern deck.

---

## How to Play

On your turn you will play cards from your hand to deal damage to the current enemy in order to defeat it. The values on the cards determine damage, while the suit provides a special power.

Each turn has four steps:

1. **Step 1 - Play a card**
2. **Step 2 - Activate the suit power**
3. **Step 3 - Deal damage to the enemy and check**
4. **Step 4 - Suffer damage from the enemy**

---

## Step 1: Play a Card

Play a card from your hand onto the table in front of you. The number of the card determines the attack value. So if a 7 of Hearts is played, the attack value is 7.

### Playing a Card

Playing a card to damage the enemy also grants a power associated with the suit of that card. Powers from red suits are resolved straight away, powers from black suits take effect in later steps.

**Note:** Suit powers are mandatory and cannot be skipped.

---

## Step 2: Activate the Suit Power

### Hearts

**Heal from the discard:** Shuffle the discard pile then count out a number of cards facedown equal to the attack value played. Place them under the Tavern deck (no peeking!) then, return the discard pile to the table, faceup. This effectively cycles cards from the discard back into play.

### Diamonds

**Draw cards:** Draw cards from the Tavern deck until a number of cards equal to the attack value played have been drawn. You may never draw cards over your maximum hand size of 8. There is no penalty for failing to draw cards from an empty Tavern deck.

### Clubs

**Double damage:** During Step 3, damage dealt by clubs counts for double. E.g., The 8 of Clubs deals 16 damage.

### Spades

**Shield against enemy attack:** During Step 4, reduce the attack value of the current enemy by the attack value played. The shield effects of spades are cumulative for all spades played against this enemy and remain in effect until the enemy is defeated.

---

## Step 3: Deal Damage and Check

Deal damage equal to the attack value of the played card to the enemy. Check to see if the total damage dealt so far is equal to or greater than the enemy's health.

### Enemy Stats

| Enemy | Attack | Health |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Jack  | 10     | 20     |
| Queen | 15     | 30     |
| King  | 20     | 40     |

**Example:** If facing a Jack and you deal 9 damage in one turn, then 12 damage in the next turn, the total damage dealt would be 21 so the enemy would be defeated.

### If the Enemy is Defeated

(i) Place the enemy in the discard pile. If you have dealt damage exactly equal to the enemy's health, place it facedown on top of the Tavern deck instead.

(ii) Place all cards played against the enemy in the discard pile.

(iii) Turn the next card of the Castle deck face up.

(iv) You skip Step 4 and begin a new turn against the enemy just revealed, starting at Step 1.

---

## Step 4: Suffer Damage from the Enemy

If not defeated, the enemy attacks you by dealing damage equal to that enemy's attack value. Remember to reduce the enemy's attack value by the total value of Spades that you have played against the enemy.

You must discard cards from your hand with a total value at least equal to the enemy's attack value. Discard the cards one at a time, faceup to the discard pile. Animal Companions have a value of 1 and the Jester a value of 0 when discarded to cover damage.

If you cannot discard enough cards to satisfy the damage, you lose the game. It's ok to have an empty hand.

---

## Enemy Immunity

Each enemy is immune to the suit powers of cards played against them which match their suit. For example, you will not draw cards when a diamond is played against the Jack of Diamonds (however the number is still added to the damage total). A Jester can be played to cancel an enemy's immunity - the suit power will then take effect normally (e.g., playing Diamond against Jack of Diamonds after using a Jester allows you to draw cards).

---

## Animal Companions

In Step 1, Animal Companions can be played on their own, but may also be paired with one other card (except the Jester). Animal Companions count as 1 towards the attack total and their suit power is also applied.

**Example:** When playing the 8 of Diamonds with the Animal Companion of Clubs the attack value is 9 and the effects of both suit powers are applied for 9: 9 cards are drawn and 18 damage is dealt.

Any time where both a Hearts power and Diamonds power are resolved together, resolve the Hearts healing before drawing with Diamonds.

Animal Companions can also be paired with one other Animal Companion. If you play an Animal Companion with another card of the same suit, you only apply the suit power once.

---

## The Jester

In Step 1 when playing a card the Jester may be played (always on its own).

The Jester has an attack value of 0. The power of the Jester is that it negates the immunity of the enemy meaning the suit powers of cards with the same suit as the enemy will now take effect when played.

After playing the Jester, skip steps 3 and 4 then begin your next turn.

### Using Jester Power (Solo)

A Jester can be flipped to activate the following power: **"Discard your hand and refill to 8 cards - this does not count as drawing for the purpose of enemy diamond immunity."**

- Since you have two Jesters this can be done twice per game.
- Flipping the Jesters in this way does not cancel enemy immunity.
- You are allowed to use the Jester power:
  - At the start of Step 1 before you play a card
  - At the start of Step 4 before you have to take damage

---

## Combos

In Step 1, instead of playing a single card, you can combine cards together in sets of 2, 3 or 4 of the same number as long as the combined total of the cards played equals 10 or less.

Animal Companions cannot be added to a combo or played as a combo on their own; they can only ever be paired with one other card (which could be another Animal Companion).

So you can play a pair of 2s, 3s, 4s, or 5s, triple 2s and 3s, or quadruple 2s. When these cards are played together all suit powers are resolved at the total attack value.

**Example:** If you play the 3 of Diamonds, Spades and Clubs together, you will draw 9 cards, reduce the enemy's attack value by 9 and deal 18 damage.

Any time where both a Hearts power and Diamonds power are resolved together, resolve the Hearts healing before drawing with Diamonds.

---

## Drawing a Defeated Enemy

Jacks in hand count as a 10, Queens in hand count as a 15 and Kings in hand count as a 20. These values are applied when either playing them as an attack card or discarding them from hand to suffer damage. Their suit power is applied as normal when played.

Enemy cards enter the Tavern deck when you defeat them with exactly their health (not overkill). In this case, the enemy is placed facedown on top of the Tavern deck and will be drawn the next time cards are drawn from the deck.

---

## Game End

The game ends when:

- **You win** by defeating the last King
- **You lose** because you are unable to satisfy the damage dealt by an enemy
- **You lose** if you are unable to play a card or flip a Jester on your turn

---

## Victory Levels

- **Bronze Victory:** A win having used 2 Jesters
- **Silver Victory:** A win having used 1 Jester
- **Gold Victory:** A win having used 0 Jesters
